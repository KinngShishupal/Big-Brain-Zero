Call Me Dumb · Android game

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and we don’t ask for your name, email, phone number or anything else about you.
  • Your scores and settings are saved only on your phone. They are never sent to us.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google’s ad software uses your device’s advertising ID and basic device information to show and measure ads.
  • You can limit ad personalisation in your phone’s settings, and in the EU, UK and Switzerland you choose this through a consent form.

This policy explains what information the Call Me Dumb game (“the app”, “we”, “us”) handles, why, and the choices you have. The app is published on Google Play by Shishupal Singh.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app has no sign-up or login, and it doesn’t ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Saved on your device only

To remember your progress, the app stores a small amount of game data on your phone:

  • your lowest IQ score and the number of games you’ve played,
  • your settings (language, music, sound effects and vibration),
  • whether you’ve already seen the “How to play” screen.

This data never leaves your device and we can’t see it. You can erase your stats at any time in Settings → Reset stats, and uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is paid for by ads served through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. The app shows a small banner, an occasional full-screen ad between games, and optional video ads you can choose to watch (for example, to retry a question). When ads are loaded, Google’s Mobile Ads software on your device may collect and process:

Information Used for
Advertising ID (a resettable ID provided by Android) Showing ads, limiting how often you see the same ad, measuring ad performance, and personalised ads where you allow them
IP address and the approximate location derived from it Choosing ads relevant to your country or region, and preventing fraud
Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version) Delivering ads that work on your device, analytics and troubleshooting
Ad interactions (which ads were shown, viewed or tapped) Ad reporting, rewarding you for videos you watch, and fraud prevention

This information is collected and handled by Google under its own policies. We don’t receive it in a way that identifies you personally. To learn more, see the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for general audiences (maximum rating “PG”).

Your choices

Ad consent in the EU, UK and Switzerland

If you’re in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google’s consent form the first time it starts, so you can choose whether your data may be used for personalised ads. You can change your choice later in Settings → Privacy and ad choices.

Advertising ID and personalised ads

On most Android phones you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Google → Ads (on some phones, Settings → Privacy → Ads). You’ll still see ads, but they won’t be based on your interests.

Using the app without ads data

Watching video ads is always optional. Turning off your internet connection stops ads from loading, and the game remains playable.

Permissions the app uses

  • Internet and network state: to load ads.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID and Android ad services permissions): used by Google AdMob as described above.
  • Vibration: for the game’s haptic feedback. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Wake lock and background work: used by Google’s ad software to complete short tasks such as ad reporting.

The app does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files or GPS location.

Sharing and selling of data

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share any data with anyone other than Google AdMob for the advertising described above.

Children

Call Me Dumb is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We don’t knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we’ll help remove it.

Data security and retention

Your game data stays on your device and is removed when you reset your stats or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept and protected according to Google’s privacy policy.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we’ll update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s Google Play listing.
